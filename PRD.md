# Product Requirements Document (PRD): PrepLoop

## 1. Overview & Vision

**Product Name**: PrepLoop
**Tagline**: The modern, full-stack tech interview preparation platform featuring AI-powered coaching and realistic interview simulations.

**Vision**: To empower job seekers in the tech industry with a unified, intelligent platform that seamlessly bridges the gap between raw technical practice (DSA/System Design) and actual interview execution. PrepLoop acts as a personal AI interviewer, coding environment, progress tracker, and community hub to help candidates land their dream jobs.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- **Software Engineering Candidates**: Freshers to senior developers preparing for technical interviews.
- **Career Switchers**: Professionals transitioning into tech who need structured paths and realistic mock interviews.
- **Tech Recruiters/Educators**: Organizations looking for a structured platform to test or train candidates.

---

## 3. Core Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)

- **Objective 1**: Provide an ultra-realistic, low-latency AI interview experience.
  - _Key Result_: Maintain conversational voice latency below 800ms using advanced Pipecat/WebSocket setups.
  - _Key Result_: Support natural conversation flows, including interruption handling and silence detection.
- **Objective 2**: Deliver a comprehensive and intuitive technical prep environment.
  - _Key Result_: Implement robust in-browser code execution playgrounds.
  - _Key Result_: Curate and categorize expansive DSA and System Design modules by company and pattern.
- **Objective 3**: Build a sustainable product ecosystem.
  - _Key Result_: Integrated Razorpay payment gates for premium features.
  - _Key Result_: Engaged community through Discord Bot integrations and site blogs.

---

## 4. Product Features & Requirements

### Epic 1: Technical Practice Workflows

- **DSA Playgrounds**: In-browser coding environments where users can practice Data Structures and Algorithms. Requires syntax highlighting, execution, and test cases validation.
- **System Design Modules**: Interactive guides and canvases to practice distributed system architecture.
- **Company-Specific Question Banks**: Datasets containing frequently asked questions segmented by top tech companies.

### Epic 2: AI-Powered Coaching & Interview Simulation

- **Conversational AI Interviewer**: A real-time voice and text-based AI agent to conduct mock interviews (Frontend UI -> Backend WebSockets -> Pipecat AI bot).
- **Silence & Nuance Detection**: Handle user pauses naturally. If silence exceeds 5-10 seconds, prompt the user; if it exceeds 15 seconds, intelligently move on or auto-submit.
- **Customizable AI Personas**: Male ("Austin") and female voices tailored via dynamic TTS providers (e.g., Groq Orpheus, ElevenLabs, Deepgram).
- **Post-Interview Feedback**: Detailed scorecard on technical accuracy, communication skills, and areas of improvement generated by LLM analysis.

### Epic 3: User Profiles & Progress Tracking

- **Email Verification & Auth**: Secure signup/login using Supabase Auth with custom SMTP email verification flows (via Brevo/Nodemailer).
- **Activity Tracking**: Dashboard displaying completed modules, interview scores, and consistency streaks.
- **Personal Notes**: Built-in markdown-friendly note-taking associated with specific problems or interviews.

### Epic 4: Community, Content, and Job Boards

- **Job Board Integration**: Live feed of tech job updates aggregated via RapidAPI/Adzuna.
- **Community Forum & Discord Integration**: Seamless linking to a Discord community, managed by a custom Discord Bot handling onboarding and community moderation.
- **Blog Content**: High-quality editorial content regarding interview strategies.

### Epic 5: Monetization

- **Premium Subscriptions / Paywalls**: Powered by Razorpay, unlocking specific company datasets, unlimited AI mock interviews, or premium voice profiles.

## 5. Technical Architecture & Constraints

### 5.1 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React 18, Vite 5, React Router, Tailwind CSS, Mantine UI, React Flow, Supabase JS.
- **Backend**: Node.js (ES Modules), Express, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Nodemailer, Razorpay SDK.
- **AI & Voice Layer**: Groq SDK for LLMs, Deepgram/ElevenLabs/Orpheus for TTS/STT, Pipecat framework (Python bot spawned by Node via WebSockets) for real-time WebRTC/WebSocket streaming.
- **Integrations**: RapidAPI, Adzuna (Jobs), Brevo (SMTP).

### 5.2 System Constraints & Performance

- **Latency**: Voice components must operate with minimal latency to preserve the illusion of a human conversational partner.
- **Concurrency**: Backend Express server must elegantly handle scaling WebSocket connections for concurrent AI interviews.
- **Security**: Strict payload limits (10mb), general and route-specific (auth) rate limiting via `helmet` and custom middlewares. All private data secured with Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).

## 7. API Structure (Backend Routes)

The backend service (`http://localhost:5000/api`) encapsulates application business logic through a robust grouping of endpoints.

### Authentication & User Management

- `/api/auth`: Login, registration, token refreshes, and email verification. Includes strict rate-limiting (30/15min).
- `/api/user`: User profile fetching and updating.
- `/api/resume`: Resume uploading and parsing features.
- `/api/activity`: Tracks user consistency streaks and progress on modules.
- `/api/notes`: Operations for personal markdown-friendly study notes.

### Technical Preparation Modules

- `/api/dsa` & `/api/practice`: Managing DSA questions, executing playground code, and verifying test cases.
- `/api/system-design`: System design preparation content and canvases.
- `/api/company-interview`: Serving curated question banks mapped to specific tech companies.

### AI & Interview Simulation

- `/api/ai` & `/api/ai-features`: General AI tools and helpers powered by Groq.
- `/api/ai/coach`: Interactive AI coaching and feedback.
- `/api/ai/interview` & `/api/interview`: Legacy AI interview flows.
- `/api/ai/interview/v2` & `/api/interview-suite`: Enhanced AI interview variants with structured scoring.
- `/api/real-interview` & `/api/schedule`: Live peer-to-peer interview scheduling or real human rounds.
- `/api/voice`: TTS and STT wrappers for bridging frontend audio (via Deepgram/ElevenLabs/Orpheus).
- `/api/pipecat`: Connects frontend WebSocket streams to the Pipecat Python-based AI Voice interviewer agent.
- `/api/analytics/overview` & `/api/recommendations`: Fetching post-interview analytics and tailored improvement advice.

### Community & Third-Party

- `/api/community` & `/api/chat`: Interactions tied to the Discord community or internal messaging.
- `/api/blog`: Serving editorial content.
- `/api/jobs`: Wrapping external job APIs (RapidAPI/Adzuna) to serve tech job listings.
- `/api/contact`: Support forms and inquiries.

### Platform Services

- `/api/payment`: Handles subscriptions, premium unlocks, and Razorpay webhooks.
- `/api/coins`: Manages virtual currency or credits for premium site actions.
- `/api/admin` & `/api/library` & `/api/hr`: Administrative functions, role checking, HR configurations, and managing premium library resources.

**Security Overview**: All APIs are protected by Helmet, general rate limiting (250/15min), JSON payload limits (10mb), and CORS strategies restricted to known origins. Route authorization relies on JWTs (`authenticateToken`) and Role-Based Access Control (`requireAdmin`).

## 8. Database Structure (Supabase / PostgreSQL)

PrepLoop utilizes Supabase, establishing its data on top of PostgreSQL. The following outlines the core schema driving the application.

### Core Architecture & User Data

- **`auth.users`** (Supabase native): Manages secure credentials and login methods.
- **`profiles`**: Extends `auth.users` to store application-specific data. Includes `full_name`, `subscription_tier`, `experience_level`, `bio`, `skills`, and `education`. Auto-created via database triggers upon user sign-up.

### DSA & Technical Prep

- **`patterns`**: Core categories traversing coding challenges (e.g., "Two Pointers", "Sliding Window") containing descriptions, theory, and difficulty.
- **`problems`**: Individual DSA questions linked to `patterns`. Contains `title`, `description`, `constraints`, `examples`, `starter_code`, and `test_cases`.
- **`user_progress`**: Tracks an individual user's completion status per problem (`attempts`, `solved_at`, `best_time_complexity`).
- **`submissions`**: A history of code submissions tracking `code`, `language`, execution metrics (`execution_time`, `memory_used`), and `ai_feedback`.

### AI Simulation & Feedback

- **`mock_interviews`**: Parent records of interview sessions storing `interview_type`, `duration`, aggregated `questions`/`responses`, and composite metrics (`communication_score`, `technical_score`).
- **`interview_feedback`**: Child records storing localized AI feedback for specific questions/answers occurring during an interview.
- **`resume_analyses`**: Stores uploaded resumes and LLM-generated reviews, including `ats_score`, `strengths`, `weaknesses`, and `keyword_matches`.

### Community & Tooling

- **`community_posts` & `community_replies`**: Facilitates the discussion forums, tracking content, likes, tags, and authors.
- **`code_snippets`**: A personal repository for users to save functions and boilerplate.

**Security Overview**: Row Level Security (RLS) is heavily enforced. By default, users can only read, insert, or update rows matching their own `user_id`. Certain tables (like `community_posts` and `patterns`) utilize policies that allow broader public read access.

## 9. Authentication & Security Structure

Authentication operates through a custom wrap of **Supabase Auth** mixed with native SMTP operations to control the email verification flow strictly within the platform.

### Registration & Login Flow

1. **Signup**: Users submit data to `/api/auth/signup`. The backend uses `supabaseAdmin` to create the user with `email_confirm: false`.
2. **Token Generation**: An internal token is generated, stored in the `profiles` table (`verification_token`, `token_expires_at`), and an email is dispatched via an configured SMTP relay (e.g., Brevo) using **Nodemailer**.
3. **Verification**: Users click the link mapping to the frontend `/verify-email`. The frontend posts to `/api/auth/verify-email`, flipping `profiles.email_verified` to `true` and allowing subsequent logins.
4. **Login**: Authenticates via Supabase `signInWithPassword`. Only proceeds if the `profiles` check confirms the email has been verified. Returns a JWT and refresh token.

### Middleware & Protection

- **`authenticateToken`**: Extracts and validates the JWT against Supabase's `getUser()` method for protected routes.
- **`requireAdmin`**: Scrutinizes the user's `profiles.role` column, granting access only if set to `'admin'`.
- **`authLimiter`**: A strict global rate limiter (30 requests / 15 minutes) bound exclusively to all `/api/auth` routes to deter brute-force attacks.
- **Action-Specific Limits**:
  - `verificationLimiter` & `forgotPasswordLimiter` enforce 3 requests per 15 minutes via IP.
  - Sub-limits (`isEmailCoolingDown`) prevent email enumeration and spam by enforcing a 60-second cooldown _per email address_.

## 10. AI Implementation Structure

PrepLoop leverages advanced LLMs (predominantly Groq) to deliver real-time feedback and simulate interactive interviews. The AI orchestration is split into several distinct operational areas.

### AI Code Review (`/api/ai-features/code-review`)

- **Submission Analysis**: Users submit code snippets mapped to specific DSA problems. The AI generates contextual feedback spanning identifying constraints and logic flaws.
- **Scoring Dimensions**: Responses are parsed and scored across `code_quality_score`, `efficiency_score`, `readability_score`, and `overall_score`. It also maps detected algorithmic strategies (like "two-pointer").
- **Complexity Extraction**: Predicts Big-O Time (`O(n)`) and Space complexity automatically against submitted solutions.

### AI Interview Simulation (`/api/ai-features/interview`)

Interviews can be initialized for DSA, System Design, or Behavioral questions, with varying difficulties and targeted company focuses. The orchestration occurs in two distinct execution modes:

1. **Hybrid Rollout (`hybrid_rollout`)**:
   - Uses traditional HTTP pipelines.
   - A multi-turn request/response flow where the AI tracks conversation history, asks follow-up questions, and evaluates candidate responses asynchronously.
2. **Full Realtime (`full_realtime`)**:
   - Initiates an ephemeral session descriptor bridged to a Python **Pipecat** instance.
   - Front-end establishes a WebSocket connection allowing sub-second conversational voice latency. Voice Activity Detection (VAD) handles interruptions gracefully.

### Scoring & Analytics (`/api/ai-features/performance-trends`)

- **Continuous Evaluation**: During an interview, individual user statements are captured in `interview_feedback_history` for micro-corrections.
- **Final Completion**: Ending a session triggers a concluding analysis compiling `communication_score`, `problem_solving_score`, and `technical_depth_score`, outputting explicit _strengths_ and _areas for improvement_.
- **Trend Aggregation**: Queries track longitudinal metric progress, showing a user their moving averages over rolling sets of completed interviews.

**Database Models Supporting AI Data**: `code_review_sessions`, `interview_sessions`, `interview_feedback_history`, `code_review_improvements`, and `interview_performance_trends`.
