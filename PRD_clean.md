# Product Requirements Document (PRD): PrepLoop

## 1. Overview & Vision

**Product Name**: PrepLoop  **Tagline**: The modern, full-stack tech interview
preparation platform featuring AI-powered coaching and realistic interview
simulations.

**Vision**: To empower job seekers in the tech industry with a unified,
intelligent platform that seamlessly bridges the gap between raw technical
practice (DSA/System Design) and actual interview execution. PrepLoop acts as a
personal AI interviewer, coding environment, progress tracker, and community hub
to help candidates land their dream jobs.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- Software Engineering Candidates**: Freshers to senior developers preparing for
  technical interviews. - Career Switchers**: Professionals transitioning into
  tech who need structured paths and realistic mock interviews. - Tech
  Recruiters/Educators**: Organizations looking for a structured platform to
  test or train candidates.

---

## 3. Core Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)

- Objective 1**: Provide an ultra-realistic, low-latency AI interview
  experience. _Key Result_: Maintain conversational voice latency below 800ms
  using a generic WebSocket-based realtime bridge. _Key Result_: Support natural
  conversation flows, including interruption handling and silence detection. -
  Objective 2**: Deliver a comprehensive and intuitive technical prep
  environment. _Key Result_: Implement robust in-browser code execution
  playgrounds. _Key Result_: Curate and categorize expansive DSA and System
  Design modules by company and pattern. - Objective 3**: Build a sustainable
  product ecosystem. _Key Result_: Integrated Razorpay payment gates for premium
  features. _Key Result_: Engaged community through Discord Bot integrations and
  site blogs.

---

## 4. Product Features & Requirements

### Epic 1: Technical Practice Workflows

- DSA Playgrounds**: In-browser coding environments where users can practice
  Data Structures and Algorithms. Requires syntax highlighting, execution, and
  test cases validation. - System Design Modules**: Interactive guides and
  canvases to practice distributed system architecture. - Company-Specific
  Question Banks**: Datasets containing frequently asked questions segmented by
  top tech companies.

### Epic 2: AI-Powered Coaching & Interview Simulation

- Conversational AI Interviewer**: A real-time voice and text-based AI agent to
  conduct mock interviews (Frontend UI -> Backend WebSockets -> realtime voice
  bridge). - Silence & Nuance Detection**: Handle user pauses naturally. If
  silence exceeds 5-10 seconds, prompt the user; if it exceeds 15 seconds,
  intelligently move on or auto-submit. - Customizable AI Personas**: Male
  ("Austin") and female voices tailored via dynamic TTS providers (e.g., Groq
  Orpheus, ElevenLabs, Deepgram). - Post-Interview Feedback**: Detailed
  scorecard on technical accuracy, communication skills, and areas of
  improvement generated by LLM analysis.

### Epic 3: User Profiles & Progress Tracking

- Email Verification & Auth**: Secure signup/login using Supabase Auth with
  custom SMTP email verification flows (via Brevo/Nodemailer). - Activity
  Tracking**: Dashboard displaying completed modules, interview scores, and
  consistency streaks. - Personal Notes**: Built-in markdown-friendly note-
  taking associated with specific problems or interviews.

### Epic 4: Community, Content, and Job Boards

- Job Board Integration**: Live feed of tech job updates aggregated via
  RapidAPI/Adzuna. - Community Forum & Discord Integration**: Seamless linking
  to a Discord community, managed by a custom Discord Bot handling onboarding
  and community moderation. - Blog Content**: High-quality editorial content
  regarding interview strategies.

### Epic 5: Monetization

- Premium Subscriptions / Paywalls**: Powered by Razorpay, unlocking specific
  company datasets, unlimited AI mock interviews, or premium voice profiles.

## 5. Technical Architecture & Constraints

### 5.1 Tech Stack

- Frontend**: React 18, Vite 5, React Router, Tailwind CSS, Mantine UI, React
  Flow, Supabase JS. - Backend**: Node.js (ES Modules), Express, Supabase
  (PostgreSQL), Nodemailer, Razorpay SDK. - AI & Voice Layer**: Groq SDK for
  LLMs, Deepgram/ElevenLabs/Orpheus for TTS/STT, a WebSocket-based realtime
  voice bridge for streaming. - Integrations**: RapidAPI, Adzuna (Jobs), Brevo
  (SMTP).

### 5.2 System Constraints & Performance

- Latency**: Voice components must operate with minimal latency to preserve the
  illusion of a human conversational partner. - Concurrency**: Backend Express
  server must elegantly handle scaling WebSocket connections for concurrent AI
  interviews. - Security**: Strict payload limits (10mb), general and route-
  specific (auth) rate limiting via `helmet` and custom middlewares. All private
  data secured with Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).

## 7. API Structure (Backend Routes)

The backend service (`http://localhost:5000/api`) encapsulates application
business logic through a robust grouping of endpoints.

### Authentication & User Management

- `/api/auth`: Login, registration, token refreshes, and email verification.
  Includes strict rate-limiting (30/15min). - `/api/user`: User profile fetching
  and updating. - `/api/resume`: Resume uploading and parsing features. -
  `/api/activity`: Tracks user consistency streaks and progress on modules. -
  `/api/notes`: Operations for personal markdown-friendly study notes.

### Technical Preparation Modules

- `/api/dsa` & `/api/practice`: Managing DSA questions, executing playground
  code, and verifying test cases. - `/api/system-design`: System design
  preparation content and canvases. - `/api/company-interview`: Serving curated
  question banks mapped to specific tech companies.

### AI & Interview Simulation

- `/api/ai` & `/api/ai-features`: General AI tools and helpers powered by Groq.
  - `/api/ai/coach`: Interactive AI coaching and feedback. - `/api/ai/interview`
  & `/api/interview`: Legacy AI interview flows. - `/api/ai/interview/v2` &
  `/api/interview-suite`: Enhanced AI interview variants with structured
  scoring. - `/api/real-interview` & `/api/schedule`: Live peer-to-peer
  interview scheduling or real human rounds. - `/api/voice`: TTS and STT
  wrappers for bridging frontend audio (via Deepgram/ElevenLabs/Orpheus). -
  `/api/voice/realtime`: Connects frontend WebSocket streams to the realtime AI
  Voice interviewer bridge. - `/api/analytics/overview` &
  `/api/recommendations`: Fetching post-interview analytics and tailored
  improvement advice.

### Community & Third-Party

- `/api/community` & `/api/chat`: Interactions tied to the Discord community or
  internal messaging. - `/api/blog`: Serving editorial content. - `/api/jobs`:
  Wrapping external job APIs (RapidAPI/Adzuna) to serve tech job listings. -
  `/api/contact`: Support forms and inquiries.

### Platform Services

- `/api/payment`: Handles subscriptions, premium unlocks, and Razorpay webhooks.
  - `/api/coins`: Manages virtual currency or credits for premium site actions.
  - `/api/admin` & `/api/library` & `/api/hr`: Administrative functions, role
  checking, HR configurations, and managing premium library resources.

**Security Overview**: All APIs are protected by Helmet, general rate limiting
(250/15min), JSON payload limits (10mb), and CORS strategies restricted to known
origins. Route authorization relies on JWTs (`authenticateToken`) and Role-
Based Access Control (`requireAdmin`).

## 8. Database Structure (Supabase / PostgreSQL)

PrepLoop utilizes Supabase, establishing its data on top of PostgreSQL. The
following outlines the core schema driving the application.

### Core Architecture & User Data

- `auth.users`** (Supabase native): Manages secure credentials and login
  methods. - `profiles`**: Extends `auth.users` to store application-specific
  data. Includes `full_name`, `subscription_tier`, `experience_level`, `bio`,
  `skills`, and `education`. Auto-created via database triggers upon user sign-
  up.

### DSA & Technical Prep

- `patterns`**: Core categories traversing coding challenges (e.g., "Two
  Pointers", "Sliding Window") containing descriptions, theory, and difficulty.
  - `problems`**: Individual DSA questions linked to `patterns`. Contains
  `title`, `description`, `constraints`, `examples`, `starter_code`, and
  `test_cases`. - `user_progress`**: Tracks an individual user's completion
  status per problem (`attempts`, `solved_at`, `best_time_complexity`). -
  `submissions`**: A history of code submissions tracking `code`, `language`,
  execution metrics (`execution_time`, `memory_used`), and `ai_feedback`.

### AI Simulation & Feedback

- `mock_interviews`**: Parent records of interview sessions storing
  `interview_type`, `duration`, aggregated `questions`/`responses`, and
  composite metrics (`communication_score`, `technical_score`). -
  `interview_feedback`**: Child records storing localized AI feedback for
  specific questions/answers occurring during an interview. -
  `resume_analyses`**: Stores uploaded resumes and LLM-generated reviews,
  including `ats_score`, `strengths`, `weaknesses`, and `keyword_matches`.

### Community & Tooling

- `community_posts` & `community_replies`**: Facilitates the discussion forums,
  tracking content, likes, tags, and authors. - `code_snippets`**: A personal
  repository for users to save functions and boilerplate.

**Security Overview**: Row Level Security (RLS) is heavily enforced. By default,
users can only read, insert, or update rows matching their own `user_id`.
Certain tables (like `community_posts` and `patterns`) utilize policies that
allow broader public read access.

## 9. Authentication & Security Structure

Authentication operates through a custom wrap of **Supabase Auth** mixed with
native SMTP operations to control the email verification flow strictly within
the platform.

### Registration & Login Flow

1. **Signup**: Users submit data to `/api/auth/signup`. The backend uses
  `supabaseAdmin` to create the user with `email_confirm: false`. 2. **Token
  Generation**: An internal token is generated, stored in the `profiles` table
  (`verification_token`, `token_expires_at`), and an email is dispatched via an
  configured SMTP relay (e.g., Brevo) using **Nodemailer**. 3. **Verification**:
  Users click the link mapping to the frontend `/verify-email`. The frontend
  posts to `/api/auth/verify-email`, flipping `profiles.email_verified` to
  `true` and allowing subsequent logins. 4. **Login**: Authenticates via
  Supabase `signInWithPassword`. Only proceeds if the `profiles` check confirms
  the email has been verified. Returns a JWT and refresh token.

### Middleware & Protection

- `authenticateToken`**: Extracts and validates the JWT against Supabase's
  `getUser()` method for protected routes. - `requireAdmin`**: Scrutinizes the
  user's `profiles.role` column, granting access only if set to `'admin'`. -
  `authLimiter`**: A strict global rate limiter (30 requests / 15 minutes) bound
  exclusively to all `/api/auth` routes to deter brute-force attacks. - Action-
  Specific Limits: - `verificationLimiter` & `forgotPasswordLimiter` enforce 3
  requests per 15 minutes via IP. - Sub-limits (`isEmailCoolingDown`) prevent
  email enumeration and spam by enforcing a 60-second cooldown _per email
  address_.

## 10. AI Implementation Structure

PrepLoop leverages advanced LLMs (predominantly Groq) to deliver real-time
feedback and simulate interactive interviews. The AI orchestration is split into
several distinct operational areas.

### AI Code Review (`/api/ai-features/code-review`)

- Submission Analysis**: Users submit code snippets mapped to specific DSA
  problems. The AI generates contextual feedback spanning identifying
  constraints and logic flaws. - Scoring Dimensions**: Responses are parsed and
  scored across `code_quality_score`, `efficiency_score`, `readability_score`,
  and `overall_score`. It also maps detected algorithmic strategies (like "two-
  pointer"). - Complexity Extraction**: Predicts Big-O Time (`O(n)`) and Space
  complexity automatically against submitted solutions.

### AI Interview Simulation (`/api/ai-features/interview`)

Interviews can be initialized for DSA, System Design, or Behavioral questions,
with varying difficulties and targeted company focuses. The orchestration occurs
in two distinct execution modes:

1. **Hybrid Rollout (`hybrid_rollout`)**: - Uses traditional HTTP pipelines. - A
  multi-turn request/response flow where the AI tracks conversation history,
  asks follow-up questions, and evaluates candidate responses asynchronously. 2.
  **Full Realtime (`full_realtime`)**: - Initiates an ephemeral session
  descriptor bridged to the realtime voice worker. - Front-end establishes a
  WebSocket connection allowing sub-second conversational voice latency. Voice
  Activity Detection (VAD) handles interruptions gracefully.

### Scoring & Analytics (`/api/ai-features/performance-trends`)

- Continuous Evaluation**: During an interview, individual user statements are
  captured in `interview_feedback_history` for micro-corrections. **Final
  Completion**: Ending a session triggers a concluding analysis compiling
  `communication_score`, `problem_solving_score`, and `technical_depth_score`,
  outputting explicit _strengths_and_areas for improvement_. **Trend
  Aggregation**: Queries track longitudinal metric progress, showing a user
  their moving averages over rolling sets of completed interviews.

**Database Models Supporting AI Data**: `code_review_sessions`,
`interview_sessions`, `interview_feedback_history`, `code_review_improvements`,
and `interview_performance_trends`.

## 11. Acceptance Criteria

- Realtime interviews**: End-to-end realtime voice interview flows (WebSocket →
  realtime voice worker → AI coach) can handle a 5-minute test session with
  <800ms median RTT for voice packets under staging load. **AI feedback**: For a
  representative sample of 200 DSA submissions, the AI `code_review` output
  matches human reviewer grading within ±1 grade level for 75%+ of samples.
  **Playground execution**: The in-browser DSA playground executes and validates
  at least 95% of curated test cases across supported languages in CI. **Auth &
  Security**: Email verification flow prevents unverified logins; RLS policies
  block cross-user reads for personal data tables in automated tests.
  **Payments**: Razorpay integration completes a sandbox subscription flow and
  receives webhook verification in staging.

Acceptance test steps (example):

- Realtime smoke**: Run a 5-minute staged realtime interview
  (`scripts/smoke/realtime_interview.js`) between a test frontend and staging
  backend; measure RTT and packet loss; owner: Engineering Lead. **AI grading
  sample**: Run `scripts/test/ai_code_review_sample.js` against 200 curated DSA
  submissions and compare aggregated scores vs. human labels; owner: ML Lead.
  **Playground CI**: Execute `npm run test --prefix frontend` for playground
  test-suite and verify 95%+ pass rate on curated cases; owner: Frontend Lead.
  **RLS & Auth**: Run backend smoke auth scripts (`scripts/smoke/auth_smoke.js`)
  validating RLS prevents cross-user reads; owner: Security/Engineering.
  **Payments**: Trigger Razorpay sandbox purchase flow and verify webhook
  processing in staging; owner: Engineering Lead.

## 13. Success Metrics & KPIs

- Weekly Active Users (WAU) — target: 10k in first 6 months - Interview sessions
  started per user — target: 2+ monthly for active cohort - Conversion rate
  free→premium — target: 3–5% - Average interview NPS — target: 40+ - Retention:
  30-day retention > 20%

Definitions & measurement notes:

- **WAU**: Unique authenticated users who initiate at least one session
  (playground, interview, or activity) within a 7-day window. Measured via
  `/api/analytics/overview` weekly aggregation. - **Interview sessions started
  per user**: Count of distinct `mock_interviews` or `interview_sessions`
  started per user in a 30-day rolling window. - **Conversion rate
  (free→premium)**: Percentage of users who upgrade to a paid tier within 30
  days of account creation (tracked via `profiles.subscription_tier` changes). -
  **Average interview NPS**: Measured via short post-interview survey
  immediately after session completion; NPS aggregated monthly.

## 15. Risks & Mitigations

- Risk: Voice latency degrades UX under load — Mitigation: autoscale realtime
  voice workers and add a fallback low-bandwidth TTS pipeline. - Risk: LLM
  feedback inconsistency — Mitigation: hybrid human-in-the-loop review for first
  N sessions per user and model calibration. - Risk: Data privacy
  (resumes/interview transcripts) — Mitigation: strict encryption-at-rest,
  truncated transcript retention, and user-controlled deletion. - Risk: Payment
  disputes and fraud — Mitigation: Razorpay webhook validation, server-side
  receipt verification, and audit logs.

### 15.1 Data Retention & Compliance

- **Default transcript retention**: 30 days after session completion unless user
  explicitly opts in for research/analytics. - **Research opt-in**: Users may
  opt-in to retain transcripts for up to 12 months to support product
  improvements; explicit consent required during onboarding or post-session
  prompt. - **Export & deletion**: Provide endpoints for export
  (`/api/user/data/export`) and deletion (`/api/user/data/delete`). Deletion
  requests trigger background jobs to purge associated `mock_interviews`,
  `interview_feedback`, and audio blobs within 48 hours. - **Encryption &
  keys**: All PII, audio, and transcripts encrypted at-rest (AES-256). Key
  rotation scheduled quarterly; backups encrypted and access-audited. - **DSAR
  contact**: DSAR / privacy requests handled via privacy@preploop.example
  (placeholder). Maintain audit trail for all data access.
